Semester

Please read the statements below and agree to them before you register.

Tuition and Fee Statement

When you register for classes, you are responsible for paying the tuition and fees for that semester. Charges go on your student account and are due by the payment deadline.

Agreement to Pay Tuition Charges Statement

You agree to pay all charges on your account. If you don't, you could get late fees and a hold that stops you from registering later. Dropping a class after the refund deadline does not remove the charges.

Interruption or Changes in Instruction due to Covid-19 Statement

If classes have to change format (for example, moving online), tuition and fees are not automatically refunded.
